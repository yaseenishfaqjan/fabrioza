Cut and sew is true ground-up manufacturing: instead of decorating blanks, we build each garment from raw fabric using your patterns and specifications. This gives you complete control over fit, fabric, construction and detailing — the route serious brands take to create original products that stand apart. FABRIOZA offers full-package (FPP) cut and sew with a 50-piece minimum and factory-direct pricing.

What You Need to Get Started

The smoothest cut and sew projects begin with a tech pack — a document specifying measurements, construction, materials, trims and artwork. If you don't have one yet, we can help develop it from your sketches or a reference garment. From there we make patterns, produce a sample, refine the fit, and move into bulk production once you approve. This structured process is how we keep quality consistent and reduce costly mistakes.
