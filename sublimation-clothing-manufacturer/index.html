Sublimation printing infuses dye directly into the fabric, so designs become part of the garment rather than sitting on top of it. The result is vivid, full-coverage color that won't crack, peel or fade — ideal for sports jerseys, performance wear and uniforms. Because sublimation has no per-color charge, even the most complex gradients, patterns and sponsor placements cost the same as a simple design. FABRIOZA manufactures fully sublimated apparel with a 50-piece minimum and factory-direct pricing.

Why Sublimation?

Sublimation is the best decoration method when you need vibrant, durable, full-coverage color on polyester and performance fabrics. Key advantages: the print is permanent and breathable because it dyes the fibers rather than coating them; there is no weight, cracking or peeling; color and coverage are unlimited at no extra cost; and it's ideal for the photographic gradients and complex patterns common in modern sportswear. For cotton-based streetwear and bold logos on fleece, we also offer screen print, DTF and embroidery.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sublimation printing?
Sublimation infuses dye directly into polyester fabric so the design becomes part of the garment. It produces vivid, full-coverage color that will not crack, peel or fade.
Does complex artwork cost more with sublimation?
No. Sublimation has no per-color charge, so complex gradients, patterns and multiple logos cost the same as a simple design.
What is the minimum order for sublimated apparel?
Just 50 pieces, making custom sublimation accessible for teams, clubs and new brands.
Can you sublimate names and numbers on jerseys?
Yes. Individual names, numbers, crests and sponsor logos are included with our sublimated jersey and uniform production.
