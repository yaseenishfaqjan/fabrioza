The Details That Define Your Brand

We manufacture to the specifics that make streetwear feel premium: garment-dyed and acid-wash finishes, heavyweight 240–500 GSM fabrics, custom woven and rubber labels, tonal and 3D puff embroidery, oversized screen prints, DTF and HD heat transfer, plus full branded packaging. Send your tech packs or reference pieces and we develop a sample so you sign off on exactly the fabric and fit before production.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for streetwear?
Just 50 pieces per style and color — designed for limited drops and capsule collections rather than huge first orders.

Yes. We offer heavyweight 240–500 GSM fabrics with garment-dye, acid-wash and vintage finishes.
Do you handle branding and packaging?
Yes. Custom woven and rubber labels, embroidery, prints, hangtags and full branded packaging are all in-house.
Can I start with one or two styles?
Absolutely. Many brands launch with a hoodie and a tee at 50 pieces each, then scale winning styles.
