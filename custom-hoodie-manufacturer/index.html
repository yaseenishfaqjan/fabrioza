Home / Custom Hoodie Manufacturer

The hoodie is the cornerstone of nearly every clothing brand, and the quality of your hoodie is what customers remember. FABRIOZA manufactures custom hoodies from premium fleece and French terry, built to the weight, fit and finish that define elevated streetwear and contemporary brands. With a minimum order of just 50 pieces and factory-direct pricing, you can launch a signature hoodie without committing to thousands of units.

Custom Hoodie Styles We Manufacture

Heavyweight Hoodies

350–500 GSM brushed fleece for a premium, structured feel that holds its shape wash after wash.

Oversized & Boxy Fits

Drop-shoulder, boxy and cropped silhouettes built for modern streetwear cuts.

Zip-Up & Pullover

Full-zip, half-zip and classic pullover hoodies with custom drawcords, eyelets and ribbing.

Decoration Options

3D puff and flat embroidery, screen print, DTF, heat transfer, appliqué, woven patches and tonal branding.

Build Your Hoodie Exactly to Spec

Every detail is customizable: fabric weight and composition (cotton, cotton-poly, French terry, brushed fleece), fit and pattern, drawcord and eyelet style, ribbing, pocket construction, custom labels and care tags, and full branded packaging. We work from your tech packs, reference samples or sketches and develop a pre-production sample so you can approve the exact feel before bulk production.
