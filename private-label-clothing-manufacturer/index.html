Private label manufacturing lets you sell apparel under your own brand without owning a factory. FABRIOZA handles design support, sampling, production, branding and packaging, so the finished garment that reaches your customer carries your label from the inside seam to the shipping box. As a factory-direct manufacturer, we keep the minimum order at just 50 pieces per style and remove the trading-company markup that inflates most private label pricing.

What Private Label Manufacturing Includes

A true private label program is more than printing a logo on a blank. We build the full branded product:

Custom Labels & Tags

Woven labels, printed neck labels, satin tags, heat-transfer tagless labels and care/content labels to spec.

Branded Packaging

Hangtags, poly mailers, tissue paper, branded boxes, stickers and inserts for a complete unboxing experience.

OEM Production

You bring the design or tech pack; we manufacture it exactly to your specification under your brand.

ODM Development

Choose from our developed styles and customize fabric, fit, color and branding to launch faster.

OEM vs ODM — Which Is Right for You?

OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) means you provide the design and we manufacture it. This gives you complete control over fit, fabric and construction and is ideal once you have established designs or tech packs. ODM (Original Design Manufacturer) means you select and brand one of our existing developed styles, which is faster and lower-risk for new brands. FABRIOZA offers both, and many clients mix the two — launching quickly with ODM styles while developing signature OEM pieces in parallel. For a deeper comparison, read our guide on private label vs OEM vs ODM.
