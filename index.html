Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
Our MOQ starts at just 50 pieces per style per colour — one of the most accessible in the industry. Larger orders unlock volume discounts of up to 40%.
What is your typical production timeline?
Samples take 5–7 days; bulk production typically 2–4 weeks depending on quantity and complexity. Shipping is 3–7 days worldwide.
Do you offer private label and OEM services?
Yes — full private label and OEM/ODM including custom woven labels, printed tags, hangtags, poly mailers and complete branded packaging.
Which countries do you ship to?
Worldwide — USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe and the Middle East — via DHL and FedEx, with DDP, FOB and express options.
Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?
Absolutely. We recommend a sample first (5–7 days); the sample cost is credited toward your bulk order, and we remake it free if you're not satisfied.
What certifications do you have?
ISO 9001 (quality), BSCI (social standards), OEKO-TEX Standard 100 (textile safety) and WRAP (responsible production).
