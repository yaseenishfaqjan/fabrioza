Our Sportswear Manufacturing Process

  1. Quote & consultation — send your designs, tech packs or reference samples and receive a free quote within 24 hours.
  2. Sampling — we develop a pre-production sample in 5–7 days for your approval.
  3. Bulk production — approved orders go into production, typically completed in 2–4 weeks.
  4. Quality control — 4-point fabric inspection, in-line checks and AQL 2.5 final inspection.
  5. Worldwide delivery — DDP, FOB and express shipping options to your door.
